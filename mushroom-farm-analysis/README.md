# Edible Mushroom Farm — Feasibility Analysis

A practical analysis of starting a "farmers market and local supplier" scale gourmet mushroom farm: market opportunity, growing methods, capital required, day-to-day operations, sales channels, and the path to profitability.

## TL;DR

A small specialty mushroom farm — roughly **500 sq ft of fruiting space**, focused on **oyster + shiitake** with one or two premium varieties — is one of the **most capital-efficient food businesses you can start**. With ~$15,000–$30,000 of startup capital and 20–40 hours/week of labor, a focused operator can realistically reach **$80,000–$140,000 in annual revenue** with **35–55% net margins** by year 2–3.

**2026 is a structurally favorable moment to start.** The US cattle herd is at a **75-year low** (86.2M head, smallest since 1951), ground beef is at a record ~$6.70/lb (+72% since 2020), and the herd cannot meaningfully rebuild before 2028. The "blended burger" (50/50 mushroom + beef) now costs **less** per pound than all-beef while winning blind taste tests — opening a brand-new wholesale channel for small mushroom farms. See [08-beef-tailwind.md](./08-beef-tailwind.md) for the full picture.

But it is **not passive income**. It is a hands-on, hygiene-intensive perishable-food business. The two failure modes that kill most new growers are: (1) chronic contamination from poor sterile technique, and (2) inability to move product fast enough through the right sales channels before it spoils.

This analysis assumes you are a **first-time operator** building toward farmers-market and direct-to-restaurant scale (not a hobby, not a 10,000 sq ft commercial facility).

## Bottom-line numbers (target small operation)

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Fruiting room size | 400–600 sq ft |
| Production capacity | 60–120 lbs/week (3,000–6,000 lbs/year) |
| Blended retail price | $12–$16 / lb |
| Year-1 revenue | $30k–$50k |
| Year-2–3 revenue (with blend channel) | $90k–$140k |
| Startup capital | $15k–$30k |
| Break-even | Month 12–24 |
| Owner labor | 20–40 hrs/week |
| Failure rate without sterile discipline | **High** — contamination ruins entire batches |
| External tailwind (2026–2028) | US beef herd at 75-yr low; ground beef ~$7/lb; blended-burger demand rising |
